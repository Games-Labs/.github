## About

Games Labs is a personal space for academic work, Computer Science studies and software development experiments. Projects are published here with a clean presentation, serving as a portfolio and as a record of what is being learned.

It is not a company: it is a lab that keeps evolving, and the repositories reflect that process.

## Areas of interest

- **Academic projects**: assignments developed as part of my studies.
- **Software development**: applications, games and tools.
- **Computer Science**: algorithms, data structures and programming fundamentals.
- **Technology experiments**: proofs of concept and tests with new technologies.
- **Personal projects**: ideas that deserve a well-presented repository.
